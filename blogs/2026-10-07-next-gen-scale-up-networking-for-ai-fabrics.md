AI infrastructure is exploding beyond single-server boundaries. As models grow and workloads demand tightly synchronized communication across larger accelerator pools, local PCIe, CXL, and proprietary interconnects are no longer sufficient as the primary scale-up mechanism. They remain important within the server, but can create constrained compute islands across accelerator generations, system designs, and vendors.
